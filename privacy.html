Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This policy explains how LifeFlow, operated by Muhammad Amir Raza, handles information when you use our personal productivity application.

1. Information we handle

  • Account information: your email address and authentication identifiers used to create and secure your account.
  • Information you save: tasks, goals, habits, expense records, and related content you choose to enter.
  • Preferences and reminders: settings associated with app features; some reminder behavior may be handled in your browser.
  • Subscription information: when paid plans are offered, limited purchase or subscription identifiers and access status may be processed to manage your plan.
  • Technical information: basic technical data processed by the hosting, authentication, or database services needed to operate the app.

2. How information is used

We use information to provide account access, save and display your records, operate app features, respond to support requests, manage subscriptions if enabled, troubleshoot problems, and protect the service.

3. Service providers and storage

LifeFlow uses Supabase for authentication and database functionality and GitHub Pages for the public website. These providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy practices. If a payment provider is enabled, that provider will process payment details at checkout. LifeFlow does not intend to store complete payment-card numbers or security codes.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share necessary information with service providers that help operate LifeFlow, when required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, security, or integrity of users and the service.

5. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service. We use the security features available from our providers, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and requests

You may stop using LifeFlow and contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your account. We may need to verify your identity and may retain some information where required or permitted by law.

7. Children

LifeFlow is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect their personal information.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy by posting a revised version and changing the effective date. For privacy questions or requests, email pharmdaamir2121@gmail.com.
