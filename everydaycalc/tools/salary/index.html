How to use this salary calculator

Enter your pay amount and select its current period. The calculator converts it using 12 months, 52 weeks and 2,080 working hours per year.

Example calculation

An annual salary of $52,000 is $4,333.33 per month or $25 per hour using a 40-hour work week.

Frequently asked question

Does this calculate take-home pay?

No. It converts gross pay and does not deduct income tax, social contributions, benefits or other payroll deductions.

Is this calculator free?

Yes. EverydayCalc tools are free to use, work in a modern browser and do not require registration.

Are my inputs saved?

The calculation runs in your browser. The values you enter are not intentionally sent to an EverydayCalc server.
