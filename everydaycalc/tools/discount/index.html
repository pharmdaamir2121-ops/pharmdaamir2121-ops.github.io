How to use this discount calculator

Enter the original price and discount percentage. The calculator subtracts the discount from the original price and shows your savings.

Example calculation

A 20% discount on $50 saves $10, making the sale price $40.

Frequently asked question

How do you calculate a discount?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage divided by 100, then subtract that amount from the original price.

Is this calculator free?

Yes. EverydayCalc tools are free to use, work in a modern browser and do not require registration.
