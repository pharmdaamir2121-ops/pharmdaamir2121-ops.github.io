ABOUT US

Useful calculations, made simple.

EverydayCalc is a free collection of browser-based calculators for everyday questions at school, work and home.

Our approach

We aim to make calculations easy to understand. Each tool explains what information to enter and how its result is produced. Our calculators run in your browser and do not require an account.

Accuracy and limitations

We test our formulas and improve tools over time. Results are for general information and educational use; always verify important financial, academic, legal or professional decisions with an appropriate qualified source.
